Discord Bot — Privacy Policy Quadram Institute Bioscience

Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, and disclosed when users interact with this Discord bot (the "Bot"). By using the Bot, users acknowledge this Privacy Policy.

1. Operator

The Bot is operated by Quadram Institute Bioscience, Core Bioinformatics Teams (the "Operator", "we", "us", or "our").

2. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to information processed through the Bot in connection with its operation in Discord servers, channels, direct interactions, and supporting infrastructure.

This policy applies only to the Bot and not to Discord itself or any third-party services unless explicitly stated. Discord and other providers may apply their own separate privacy terms and notices.

3. Information Collected

Depending on how the Bot is configured and used, the Bot may process:

  • Discord usernames, display names, user IDs, server IDs, channel IDs, and message metadata.
  • Message content, prompts, commands, attachments, and other content submitted to the Bot.
  • Generated outputs and response history.
  • Technical and operational data such as timestamps, error logs, rate-limit events, diagnostic logs, and usage patterns.
  • System and infrastructure information necessary for security, monitoring, abuse prevention, and service reliability.

4. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to:

  • Receive and process prompts or commands sent to the Bot.
  • Generate responses and provide Bot functionality.
  • Maintain, debug, secure, and improve the Bot and its supporting systems.
  • Detect misuse, abuse, spam, unauthorized activity, or security threats.
  • Monitor performance, reliability, and service quality.
  • Comply with legal obligations or respond to lawful requests where required.

5. AI and Third-Party Processing

The Bot may use third-party infrastructure, hosting services, model-serving systems, or inference providers to process prompts and generate outputs, including language model systems such as vLLM and related components.

As a result, information submitted to the Bot may be transmitted to and processed by third-party systems to the extent necessary to provide the service. The technical architecture and providers used may change over time.

6. Lawful Basis and Intended Use

The Bot is intended for experimental and operational use within Discord environments controlled or administered by its users.

Where applicable under data protection law, processing may be carried out on the basis of legitimate interests in operating, securing, maintaining, and improving the Bot, or on another lawful basis as appropriate to the context of use.

7. Data Retention

Information may be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, including operation, troubleshooting, safety, audit, and legal compliance.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of data, system configuration, operational needs, and legal requirements. Logs and prompts may be deleted, anonymised, aggregated, or retained for limited periods at the Operator's discretion.

8. Data Sharing

Information may be shared:

  • With infrastructure, hosting, analytics, logging, or inference providers involved in operating the Bot.
  • With authorised administrators or operators of the Bot where needed for maintenance, moderation, abuse investigation, or security review.
  • Where disclosure is required by applicable law, regulation, legal process, or enforceable governmental request.
  • Where necessary to protect the rights, safety, security, systems, users, or integrity of the Operator or others.

The Bot is not intended to sell personal data or disclose it for unrelated commercial advertising purposes.

9. User Responsibilities

Users should avoid submitting confidential, proprietary, personal, health-related, regulated, sensitive, or security-critical information to the Bot unless they are satisfied that such use is appropriate and authorised in their context.

Server owners and administrators are responsible for deciding whether the Bot is suitable for their server and for providing any notices, disclosures, or governance required for their users.

10. Security

Reasonable technical and organisational measures may be used to help protect information processed by the Bot. However, no system can be guaranteed to be completely secure, and absolute security cannot be promised.

11. International Processing

Depending on the infrastructure used, information processed by the Bot may be stored or processed in jurisdictions outside the user's own country.

By using the Bot, users acknowledge that cross-border processing may occur where required for hosting, inference, support, or related technical operations.

12. Children

The Bot is not directed specifically to children. Users must comply with Discord's age requirements and any applicable legal requirements governing use of online services.

13. Rights and Requests

Where applicable under law, individuals may have rights relating to access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, or portability in respect of personal data processed through the Bot.

Any such request may be directed through the project or organisational channel used to provide access to the Bot. Fulfilling a request may depend on the ability to identify the relevant data, technical feasibility, legal obligations, and the role of third-party processors.

14. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time by publishing a revised version. Continued use of the Bot after an updated version takes effect indicates acceptance of the revised policy.

15. Contact

For privacy-related enquiries about the Bot, contact the operator through the relevant project or organisational channel used to provide access to the Bot.
