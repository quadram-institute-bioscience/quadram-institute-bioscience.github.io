# Quadram Institute Logo Assets

| Download | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| [QI-logo.png]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/branding/QI-logo.png) | **Standard full-colour stacked logo.** Dark navy square icon with teal outline frame, lime green inner square, and "Science · Health · Food · Innovation" tagline. Use on white or light backgrounds where the primary logo is needed. |
| [QI-logo-black-on-transparent.png]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/branding/QI-logo-black-on-transparent.png) | **Black monochrome stacked logo on transparent background.** Includes the icon, institute name, and tagline rendered entirely in black. Use for single-colour printing, documents, or any light background where colour is unavailable or inappropriate. |
| [QI-logo-for-dark-bg.png]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/branding/QI-logo-for-dark-bg.png) | **Colour stacked logo for dark backgrounds.** Teal outline frame and lime green square with no built-in dark fill, allowing the page background to show through. Use on dark or coloured backgrounds where the standard logo would not contrast sufficiently. |
| [QI-logo-v1-horizontal-colour.png]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/branding/QI-logo-v1-horizontal-colour.png) | **Full-colour horizontal logo.** Icon and "Quadram Institute · Science · Health · Food · Innovation" arranged in a wide landscape layout. Use on white or light backgrounds where horizontal space is available and a compact vertical footprint is preferred (e.g. website headers, banners, email signatures). |
| [QI-logo-v1-horizontal-white.png]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/branding/QI-logo-v1-horizontal-white.png) | **White horizontal logo on transparent background.** All elements rendered in white in the same wide landscape layout. Use on dark or strongly coloured backgrounds where a reversed-out white logo is required (e.g. dark navigation bars, coloured slide headers, dark-themed websites). |
