How we will use your registration information for the LLM & Python Hackathon.

Privacy note

How we will use your registration information

The information you provide in the registration form will be used to organise the LLM with Python Hackathon 2026, contact participants, allocate API credits, support project coordination, and communicate event updates.

We will only ask for information that is relevant to running the hackathon, such as your name, email address, affiliation, experience level, participation preference, and optional project idea.

Project information

Sharing project ideas

If you choose to provide a project idea, we may use it to help organise support, identify possible collaborations, and understand the range of interests among participants.

We will not publicly share your project idea, name, or affiliation without your permission. The registration form may ask whether you are happy for basic project information to be shared with other participants to encourage collaboration.

AI tools

Using LLM providers during the hackathon

During the hackathon, participants may choose to use external LLM providers such as OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, or other services available through OpenRouter.

Please do not submit confidential, personal, sensitive, unpublished, or restricted information to external AI services unless you have permission to do so and have checked the relevant data handling requirements.

Use public, synthetic, anonymised, or non-sensitive data for hackathon projects wherever possible.
API credits

OpenRouter credit allocation

If you request OpenRouter API credits, your registration details may be used to manage credit allocation and communicate instructions for accessing those credits.

Credits are intended only for participation in the hackathon and prototype development during the event.

Retention

How long we keep information

Registration information will be kept only for as long as needed to organise, run, evaluate, and follow up on the hackathon.

After the event, we may keep limited summary information, such as the number of participants and types of projects submitted, for reporting and planning future activities.

Questions

If you have concerns

If you have questions about how your information will be used, or if you need to update or withdraw your registration, please contact the event organisers.
