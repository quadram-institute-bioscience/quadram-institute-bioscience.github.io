Virtual Hackathon · 2026

Only two scheduled sessions

This is a virtual, asynchronous hackathon designed to fit around normal work. There are only two optional scheduled in-person moments: the launch in July and the showcase in September. Between these dates, you work independently, at your own pace, alone or in small groups.

Register for the hackathon

At a glance

Timeline

Attend the two in-person sessions if you can, then work on your own schedule in between.

July 2027

Registrations open

Sign up to reserve your place. If you plan to code you can get API credits. Individual participation is completely fine — teams are welcome too. Coding skills are not required, your ideas can be useful too!

27 July 2026

In-person mini tutorial (launch) In person

UG55A and B

We introduce LLMs, show how to call them from Python, and demonstrate a few simple examples using OpenAI, Anthropic Claude, and Google Gemini via OpenRouter.


On July 28th you can gather in UG55A (14:00 to 16:00) to discuss you ideas with your group partners, if you wish

July – September 2026

Independent project work Asynchronous

Build at your own pace. No mandatory meetings. Optional opportunities for questions, discussion, and sharing progress. Working alone is perfectly suitable — a good project can be as simple as one Python script that solves a real problem.

20 August 2026

Submit your ideas/prototypes Asynchronous

At this stage you might have experimented with LLMs and conceived an idea. No code is requried: share the idea with us to have the chance of getting extra OpenRouter credits.

15 September 2026

Showcase, wash-up & prizes In person

Participants briefly present what they built, what they learned, and what they might improve.

How to participate

Individual work is completely fine

You do not need to join a team. Many participants will work alone, and that is perfectly suitable for this hackathon.

Small groups are also welcome if people want to collaborate, but the event is designed so that an individual participant can complete a useful prototype.
